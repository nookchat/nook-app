Privacy policy

Nook encrypts your messages, files, names and settings on your device before it sends them. Our servers keep data that they cannot read. This page tells you what data we can see, and why.

Last changed: 13 October 2026.

What we cannot see

  • Your messages, direct messages, files, pictures and notes.
  • Your name, your picture, and the names of your spaces and channels.
  • Your list of spaces and your settings.
  • Your voice, camera and screen. These go between devices, encrypted. Nook does not record calls.

What our servers can see

DataWhy
Your IP address, and when you connectTo send data to your device, and to stop abuse.
How much data each space and each file hasTo store it, and to set limits.
A push address for your device, if you turn on notificationsTo send you a notification. The notification is encrypted, so we cannot read it.
Links that you share, if link previews are onTo show a preview card.
Words that you search for in GIF searchTo get GIFs from the GIF service.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Other services

  • Push notifications: Apple, Google, or your browser maker delivers notifications. They see when a notification goes to your device, but not what it says.
  • GIF search: the GIF service gets your search words from our server, not from your device.
  • Hosting: our servers and website run on hosting providers. They store the encrypted data.

Servers that other people run

If you use a space on a server that another person runs, that person sees the data in the table above for that server. This policy does not apply to their server.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings, Profile, Delete account. The terms of use tell you what a delete removes.
  • Leave a space: the space menu, Leave space.
  • Delete a message: the message menu, Delete.
  • Turn off notifications: Settings, Notifications.

Children

Nook is not for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 uses Nook, tell us and we remove their data.

Contact

Send questions about your privacy to support@getforeverafter.com.
